One active card sets the pace

The development subscription at Poket Dev runs on unlimited request cards over a Trello board with one hard rule: one card is Active at a time. Unlimited describes the list you can pile onto the board. The single Active slot sets the pace. At the $5,995 monthly rate, a working month of 20 weekdays prices that slot at about $300 a day. On the yearly plan at $4,995 a month the same slot-day costs about $250.

A delivery date on this workflow is queue arithmetic. Take a card sitting behind eight others that each average a day and a half in the slot: it waits 12 working days, which lands at about two and a half calendar weeks. Nothing is lost in the wait, cards hold their place and revisions run until you are satisfied, but the date is set by slot-days already promised plus your own, not by anyone's optimism.

The 72-hour turnaround promise reads the same way: it bounds a single card's time in the Active slot at three working days, not the queue ahead of it. Ten cards dropped on the board in one morning close in up to 30 working days at that bound, six weeks. The escape hatch is that canceling is allowed anytime and the finished code stays yours in the private repo, so the unused part of any month is just the cards still waiting in line. The board rules, the tiers, and the turnaround promise are at Poket Dev, same terms at poketdev.com.

The queue is the calendar.
